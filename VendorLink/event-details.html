Cape Town Summer Market

Green Point Park, Cape Town • 20 August 2026

About This Market

The Cape Town Summer Market gathers artisanal street food vendors, local handcrafted jewelry, sustainable fashion designers, and local produce producers for an energized community weekend against the backdrop of Green Point Park.

With an estimated 4,500+ attendees over the course of the day, this market provides exceptional exposure for emerging small businesses, food truck operators, and craft stallholders.
