About VendorLink

Connecting vendors and event organisers through one simple platform.

Our Story

VendorLink was created to solve a common challenge faced by vendors and event organisers. Vendors often struggle to find legitimate markets and festivals to apply for, while organisers spend countless hours searching for suitable vendors.

Our platform bridges this gap by providing one trusted place where organisers can advertise available stalls and vendors can easily discover and apply for opportunities.

Our Mission

To empower small businesses by making vendor opportunities accessible, organised and transparent.
