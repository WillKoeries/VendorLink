Bridging the Structural Gap in Local Markets

Historically, small vendors in South Africa had to discover weekend trading opportunities through fragmented WhatsApp groups, Facebook posts, and word-of-mouth. Organizers spent countless hours sifting through manual PDF forms and double-booking stalls.

VendorLink was engineered to solve this friction: delivering a single, unified platform where market hosts configure live stall capacities and local businesses can reserve spaces with verified peace of mind.
