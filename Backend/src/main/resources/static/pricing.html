Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about plans, stall fees and applications.

Are stall fees separate from the plan?

Yes. Your VendorLink plan gives you access to the application tools. Stall fees are set by each organiser and are paid to them for the events you're approved for.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

You can move between plans when your trading season changes, for example upgrading before the festive season and dropping back to the free plan afterwards.

How does stall capacity work?

Every approved application uses one stall. When the last stall is allocated, the event shows as “Fully booked” and new applications are blocked.

How do I know if my application was approved?

You'll get a notification on VendorLink as soon as the organiser decides, and you can follow every application's status on your vendor dashboard.
