VendorLink is a central platform that connects small stallholders, informal traders and festival organisers across South Africa in one transparent place.

Drag the background to explore the vendor network

Bridging the gap in local markets

Small vendors in South Africa have always found weekend trading spots through WhatsApp groups, Facebook posts and word of mouth. Organisers spend hours going through paper forms and double-booked stalls.

VendorLink puts both sides on one platform: organisers publish events with live stall numbers, and local businesses apply for stalls knowing exactly what's available.

Clear stall numbers

Every event shows how many stalls are left, updated with each approval.

No overbooking

When the last stall is allocated, the event closes to new applications.
