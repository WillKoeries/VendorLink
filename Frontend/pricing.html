Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, memberships, and stall applications.

Are stall fees separate from the subscription plan?

Yes. The VendorLink membership gives you access to digital application workflows and discovery tools. Stall fees set by event organizers are paid directly for the specific events you are approved to trade at.

Can I upgrade or cancel my membership at any time?

Absolutely. You can upgrade from the Casual Free plan to Professional whenever peak market seasons begin and pause your subscription during off-season months with zero cancellation fees.

How does the automated stall capacity rule work?

Our platform strictly prevents overbooking. When an organizer approves the final stall, the event is immediately flagged as "Fully Booked" and new applications are locked automatically.

What payment methods are supported?

We support South African debit and credit cards (Visa & Mastercard), instant EFT via Ozow and PayFast, and municipal invoice PO workflows for enterprise accounts.
